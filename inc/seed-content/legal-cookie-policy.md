This Cookie Policy explains how **OGC NewFinity** (“we”, “us”, “our”), based in The Hague, Netherlands, uses cookies and similar technologies on ogcnewfinity.com. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites function, remember preferences, and — where enabled — understand how visitors use the site in aggregate.

Similar technologies (local storage, pixels, or session identifiers) may be used for comparable purposes. In this policy, “cookies” includes those similar technologies where relevant.

## Categories of Cookies We Use

### Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for core site security and functionality. Examples on this site include:

- Session or security-related cookies supporting form submission
- Measures tied to spam protection on the Contact and Submit Your Idea forms (nonce / anti-abuse checks)

**Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent** under ePrivacy/GDPR rules applicable in the Netherlands/EU, because the site cannot operate securely without them.

### Analytics cookies (if / when enabled)

If we enable analytics tooling, it will be used for **aggregated, privacy-respecting analytics** — for example, understanding which pages are visited and how the site performs overall — not for selling personal profiles.

No specific analytics vendor is confirmed in this policy yet. When a tool is introduced, we will update this Cookie Policy with the relevant details and obtain consent where required before setting non-essential analytics cookies.

### AI Agent / future chat cookies

A floating “AI Agent” icon currently appears as a **non-functional UI placeholder**. It does not set chat session cookies today.

When the planned Amy Agent plugin becomes functional, it may set its own session cookies to operate the chat experience. This Cookie Policy will be updated at that time, and consent will be requested where legally required before any non-essential cookies are set.

## Consent

- **Strictly necessary cookies** — no consent required
- **Non-essential cookies** (such as analytics or future optional AI-chat features) — will only be set **after** you give consent through a cookie banner or equivalent control, once those technologies are introduced

Until non-essential cookies are enabled, you may browse the site without an analytics/consent banner for those categories.

## How to Control or Delete Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Typical options include blocking all cookies, blocking third-party cookies, or deleting cookies already stored.

Note: blocking strictly necessary cookies may break form submission or other core site features.

Browser help pages (examples):

- [Chrome cookie settings](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox cookie settings](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari cookie settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge cookie settings](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)
