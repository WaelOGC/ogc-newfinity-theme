# Why AI Features Need Product Owners, Not Just Prompts

**Category:** AI & Innovation  
**Audience:** Product Managers, AI Engineers, CTOs, Technical Leads

---

A common failure pattern in AI-powered products: the prompt gets iterated in isolation until it "feels good" in testing, then ships without anyone owning how it behaves in production over time. Models change, user behavior shifts, and edge cases accumulate — but nobody is watching.

## Treat the Model as a Dependency

AI features should be owned the same way any other critical dependency is owned: with clear success metrics, a defined escalation path when outputs go wrong, and a named person responsible for reviewing drift after model updates. Without that ownership, "it worked in the demo" quietly becomes "we're not sure why it's doing that" a few months into production.

Agencies building AI into client products should treat this ownership model as part of the deliverable — not an afterthought once something breaks.
